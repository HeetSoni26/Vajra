# Vajra Brand Guidelines & Visual Identity System

The official brand identity for **Vajra** — a foundation AI framework for building, training, verifying, and scaling open-source foundation models.

---

## 1. Mission & Vision

**Vajra** (Sanskrit for *thunderbolt* or *indestructible diamond*) represents strength, speed, mathematical precision, and absolute clarity. The mission of Vajra is to provide an open-source, production-grade foundation model framework with deterministic reproducibility, verifiability, and seamless scalability.

---

## 2. Logo Philosophy & Vector Geometry

The Vajra visual identity combines two core elements:
1. **The Vajra Trident Symbol**:
   - An abstract, geometric diamond and thunderbolt constructed from faceted polygonal panels (`#5B38D5`, `#6F44F0`, `#3A1F99`).
   - A central glowing cyan core (`#00F0FF` to `#0080FF`) representing energy, precision, and foundational clarity.
   - Symmetrical left and right wings forming an abstract "V" motif.
2. **The Geometric Wordmark**:
   - Modern, high-tech geometric letterforms for `V A J R A` designed with exact angular proportions and stencil crossbars.
3. **The Tagline**:
   - `BUILD. TRAIN. VERIFY. SCALE.` — rendered in wide-spaced uppercase cyan text.

## 4. Typography

- **Primary Brand Font**: Custom Geometric Vector Wordmark (`branding/wordmark.svg`).
- **UI & System Typography**: System Sans-Serif Stack (`Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`).
- **Monospace Code Font**: `JetBrains Mono`, `Fira Code`, or system `ui-monospace`.

---

## 5. Clear Space & Minimum Sizes

- **Clear Space**: Always maintain a clear margin around the logo equal to at least `0.5x` the height of the symbol.
- **Minimum Symbol Size**:
  - Web Favicon: `16x16px`
  - Print / Vector: `12mm` minimum width
- **Minimum Lockup Width**:
  - Horizontal Lockup: `180px` minimum width

---

## 6. Theme Usage (Dark & Light Mode)

- **Dark Mode (Default)**: Use `branding/logo-dark.svg` (`#060814` background, white wordmark, glowing cyan core).
- **Light Mode**: Use `branding/logo-light.svg` (white background, midnight navy wordmark, deep indigo facets).
- **Monochrome**: Use `branding/logo-monochrome.svg` for single-color print, fax, or laser engraving applications.

---

## 7. Incorrect Usage (Strict Prohibition)

- **DO NOT** stretch, distort, or skew the logo aspect ratio.
- **DO NOT** recolor the symbol facets with non-approved colors (e.g., green, yellow, red).
- **DO NOT** alter the typography of the wordmark or change letter spacing.
- **DO NOT** add drop shadows, outer glows, or complex gradients outside approved vector assets.
- **DO NOT** detach the central cyan core from the symbol wings.

---

## 8. Brand Voice & Tone

- **Authoritative & Scientific**: Grounded in empirical benchmark verifiability and reproducible metrics.
- **Clear & Minimal**: Technical accuracy without fluff or hype.
- **Developer-Centric**: Focused on performance, transparency, and production readiness.
